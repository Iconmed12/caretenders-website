Direction A, in detail

The same screen, four days apart

How the home screen behaves as your account fills up. The first one is exactly what you would see today.

What makes this different from the last attempt

  • Last time the screen laid things out in rows and hid them when empty. With one tender and no documents, almost everything hid. That is how you got a bare screen.
  • This time the screen always has a subject. A tender if there is one, a setup list if there is not, a track record either way. There is no arrangement of your current data that produces a blank page.
  • The countdown bar is the piece of craft. It costs nothing, uses dates we already hold, and turns a deadline into something felt rather than read.
  • Finish setting up earns its place. Your vault is empty and there is no company profile row yet, so today it would show one of three done. It disappears for good once all three are ticked.
