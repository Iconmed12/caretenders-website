Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your Cana Bids account and the data associated with it. Cana Bids is provided by Cana Technology Ltd.

How to request deletion

  1. Email hello@getcana.co.uk from the email address on your Cana Bids account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder and confirm once your account has been deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign in details.
  • Your company profile, evidence and saved details.
  • Your bid history and the responses and documents generated for you that we hold in our systems.

What we keep, and for how long

We keep only the limited records we are legally required to retain, for example transaction and invoice records needed for tax and accounting. These are held for up to 7 years and then deleted. Copies in routine encrypted backups are removed on our normal backup cycle, within 30 days.

How long it takes

We complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request, and email you to confirm.

Any questions about deleting your account or data? Email hello@getcana.co.uk.
