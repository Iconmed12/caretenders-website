FAQs

A few things you might be wondering.

Still have a question? Talk to the Cana team.

Contact Cana →
What types of tenders can I find?
Local authority, NHS and wider public-sector tenders across many sectors, from frameworks and dynamic purchasing systems to spot contracts, covering services, works and goods.
Do I need to find and upload all the tender questions?
No. For tenders listed on Cana, the relevant tender documents and questions are prepared on the platform so you can move directly into the response process.
Does Cana Bids write the full tender response?
Cana helps structure relevant responses using the specification, evaluation criteria and your evidence. Your team reviews, strengthens and approves every answer before submission.
Can I upload my policies and evidence?
Yes. Keep policies, plans, training matrices, case studies, company data and compliance evidence in one secure evidence library.
Can my team and consultant work on the same bid?
Yes. Assign sections to directors, managers, finance teams and tender consultants, each with the right level of access.
Can I send Cana a tender I found elsewhere?
Yes. Send the tender URL to Cana and the team can help source the opportunity and its documents and add it to the platform, subject to availability and suitability.
Does Cana submit the tender on my behalf?
Cana prepares the response package for your review. Your organisation remains responsible for checking, approving and submitting the final tender through the buyer's required process.
Is my organisation's information secure?
Your evidence and bids are held securely with role-based access, so only the people you choose can see and edit them.
