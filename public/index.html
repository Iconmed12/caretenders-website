FAQ

Care tender questions, answered

What types of care tenders can I find?
Domiciliary care, supported living, residential and nursing care, children's services, mental health, learning disabilities and autism, complex care, extra care, day opportunities, community support and care staffing frameworks from local authorities, NHS bodies and other public-sector commissioners.
Can Cana Bids help with local authority frameworks?
Yes. Cana Bids is built around local authority frameworks, dynamic purchasing systems, NHS and integrated care opportunities and spot contracts.
Does Cana Bids write the full tender response?
Cana helps structure relevant responses using the specification, evaluation criteria and your evidence. Your team reviews, strengthens and approves every answer before submission.
Can I upload my care policies and evidence?
Yes. Keep safeguarding policies, mobilisation plans, training matrices, case studies, workforce data and compliance evidence in one secure evidence library.
Can my registered manager and consultant work on the same bid?
Yes. Assign sections to directors, registered managers, clinical leads, finance teams and tender consultants, each with the right level of access.
Does Cana check CQC and compliance requirements?
Cana surfaces CQC and compliance prompts against the requirement so your team can confirm eligibility and evidence before submitting.
Can I receive alerts for specific locations?
Yes. Set your service categories and preferred locations to receive personalised local authority and NHS alerts.
Can I use Cana Bids before the mobile app launches?
Yes. The full platform is available on desktop and mobile web, with the dedicated mobile app arriving soon.
Is my organisation's information secure?
Your evidence and bids are held securely with role-based access, so only the people you choose can see and edit them.
Can I still work with a tender consultant?
Yes. Add optional care tender consultant review on any bid, or choose the Consultant Supported level for end-to-end support.
