Common questions

Do I need a membership to bid?

No. A single tender is £480, paid as you go, with no membership and no commitment. A plan is worth it once you bid regularly.

What is the difference between the plans?

Every membership includes unlimited bids delivered to your inbox, as one payment for your chosen term (3, 6 or 12 months). Access is one user. Pro covers a team of 3 and adds 2 response reviews a month and more tender requests. Gold covers 6 users and adds 2 full tender reviews a month and unlimited tender requests. Prefer not to commit? A one-off bid is £480.

How does the team work?

Everyone gets their own login and their bids go to their own email. On team plans you share one company profile and evidence library, and the account owner gets an overview of the whole team. There is no live co-editing of a single document: Cana is a central procurement hub, not a shared word processor.

Can I get the expert review on any plan?

Yes. The £350 review can be added to any bid, whether you pay as you go or are on a plan.
