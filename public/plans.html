Common questions

Do I need a membership to bid?

No. A single tender is £480, paid as you go, with no membership and no commitment. Membership is only worth it if you expect to bid regularly.

What is the difference between the £480 and the £350?

The £480 is Cana writing your full bid for one tender. The £350 is an optional extra where a human expert reviews and sharpens that bid before you submit. They are separate, and you only pay for the review if you want it.

Can I get the expert review without being a member?

Yes. The £350 review can be added to any bid, whether you paid £480 for a single tender or you are a member.

What does membership include?

Unlimited bids for your term, with everything in pay as you go on every tender, plus matched alerts for your sector. From £249 a month on the 12-month plan.

Can I cancel?

Membership is one payment per term and renews automatically at the end of the term. You can cancel any time before it renews.
